Combat Companion: Your one-stop-shop for D&D encounters

Motivation

Combat companion is an idea I've been building up over the course of multiple years to solve a particular problem: tracking player and monster health, conditions, and initiative in D&D all in one place. While there are a lot of apps on the market that do one or more of these things, I've yet to find one, self-contained app that tracks all of it. Usually it's up to the players to track what conditions they're under, or if they're concentrating on a spell. And, if the DM wants to keep track, usually it's done on paper along with notes about enemy health and conditions. So I dreamed up the Combat Companion to give DMs a one-stop-shop to combat in D&D.

The app contains a database of all the player characters, monsters, and conditions the dm needs, as well as a way to add new entries or manage existing ones. Along with that comes a database of player classes, monster types, and a few other things to help make filling out player and monster sheets easier.

Once all your data is entered, you can (eventually), use the combat tracker to add players and monsters to an encounter where all of their hp, skills, initiative, and condition data can be tracked over the course of the battle.

Current Implementation

The current version of Combat Companion includes fully functional databases for: Player Characters, Parties, Monsters, Monster Types, Player Classes, Player Species, and Conditions. Users can add, remove, review, or edit their entries using the respective database's GUI elements.

Future Plans

The next step for the app is to implement the combat tracker. This includes: developing a system to import players and monsters into an encounter, developing the GUI elements for the current and upcoming combatants, creating systems to add/remove combatants while maintaining initiative order, and implementing a system to track cumulative rewards from monsters defeated in the encounter.
