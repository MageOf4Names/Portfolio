My personal projects

Outside of my professional life, I enjoy making small apps, scripts, and other interesting (at least to me) coding projects. All of my publicly available projects can be found on My GitHub, but here are some ones that I'm especially interested in showing off.

Bear Totem Companion

The Bear Totem Companion app is a discord bot I made to help the owner of my local game store sort out MTG commander pods easier. Since the pods are pre-selected across 2 rounds, the store has some rules they follow when selecting play groups to make sure that everyone has the chance to have a good time and no one is left in too small a group or sent to a secondary seating area more than once in a night.

Combat Companion

Combat companion is a (still in progress) application meant to be a one-stop-shop for managing encounters in Dungeons and Dragons. This is my "eating an elephant one bite at a time" project, so progress is always ongoing. The current implementation is mostly just a shell for interacting with various databases. There's different databases for player classes, species, monsters, status effects, player characters, and even full adventuring parties. The next phase of development will involve tying those databases in with a fully-functioning initiative tracking system that will allow a GM to track not only initiative, but player and monster hp, available attacks, and continuous effects.

John Doe

The John Doe bot is a nifty little bot for Discord that will randomize the bare bones of a D&D character for you. Version 1.0 is finished, but I do have plans to improve the bot significantly. The current bot will allow you to randomly generate a stat sheet for a randomly-generated character including species, class, subclass, stats, and HP.

Recipe Book

Recipe Book is an Android app I made as part of an application design class. It combines my love of cooking, my passion in data, and my inability to figure out what to eat into one CRUDdy app. The bare bones of the app are there and, who knows, one day I may get around to publishing it.
