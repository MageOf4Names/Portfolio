# Recipe Book: A simple and clean way to track your recipes.

## Motivation

When I was in grad school my wife (then girlfriend) and I were constantly looking for easy meals to prepare without falling back on the same 2 or 3 recipes over and over again. She has a pretty restrictive diet, so meal services and recipe prep apps didn't really do the trick. I had tried keeping lists of things that we both liked to eat on hand, but then finding the recipes for them in my mess of bookmarks and/or screenshots folder was becoming a pain. So, I took the oportunity to make a solution myself when I saw that my mobile systems class had the option for a final project. Thus RecipeBook was born as a way to make tracking recipes simple and at arms reach.

## Current Implementation

RecipeBook in it's current form allows you to store recipes in one of 3 categories: mains, sides, and desserts. Each category varies a bit in implementation, but the general idea is that each recipe has a set of ingredients and a list of steps needed to prepare the dish. All the recipes are stored in plain text to make them easy to interact with and each category is searchable for easy access.

## Future Plans

Though the project has sat on the back-burner for a while, I do want to eventually expand RecipeBook. Potential features include: more standard ingredient formatting (a value/unit/ingredient format instead of plain text), exporting your recipe book for importing/use on other devices, a method of bundling recipes to share with others, and a meal planning feature that will measure your weekly groceries for you.
