# A Little About Me

I was born and raised in New Mexico in a little town smack dab in the middle of the state called Socorro. We're mainly known for the New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology (NMT), which is where I got both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Computer Science. Growing up, I had always had a fascination with computers. I spent many afternoons in my school's computer lab playing flash games and checking out cool programs while I waited for my dad to finish work in his office down the hall. I didn't have much access to technology aside from whatever trickled down from my siblings and our ancient home computer, but that never stopped me from being curious. By the time I got to high school, my love for video games and computers had expanded to code and systems. I remember doing Python coding tutorials on my phone long before having the chance to get a formal education in the subject. When I was a high school senior, I enrolled in my first programming class at NMT through a dual credit program at my high school. It was there that I learned Python and fell in love with programming in general.

Fast-forwarding to the present, I now reside in Spokane, Washington with my wife, our two cats, and one energetic pup (who knows, maybe there's a few pictures around here somewhere). I find myself with less time to spend on personal projects these days, but I still try to get to them when I feel inspired. Some of my projects are ways to help keep me sharp, some are ways for me to learn new skills or technologies in a way that doesn't make my eyes glaze over. And some are just funny things I wanted to exist in the world, so I made them.
